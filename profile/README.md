# APSL

**Engineering ideas into working systems.**

APSL develops engineering software, tools, and infrastructure for building and managing real-world systems.

Our work spans electronic design automation, embedded development, engineering data, and the infrastructure that connects them.

## What we're building

- **Engineering tools** — Workflows for KiCad, simulation, and engineering data.
- **Embedded systems** — Development and tooling for hardware and firmware.
- **Engineering infrastructure** — Reproducible environments, automation, CI, and release management.
- **Connected workflows** — Bringing design, inventory, procurement, and production closer together.

## Open development

We believe useful engineering tools should be understandable, maintainable, and built on clear interfaces.

Public projects and documentation will appear here as they become available.
